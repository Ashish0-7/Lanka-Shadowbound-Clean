# LANKA: SHADOWBOUND

*An island that appears on no map.*

A top-down action RPG made for **PixelForge 2026, theme: THE UNKNOWN**. Built in Godot 4.7 (GL Compatibility).

## The pitch
Your boat strikes sand where every chart shows open sea. The village is empty, its fires still warm. Crabs, gulls and monkeys have turned into hollow, glowing shadows. To find out what happened, you need to recover three shards of the Great Lamp and relight it before THE NAMELESS wakes.

## How it connects to THE UNKNOWN
- **Unexplored world:** the whole island starts covered by animated fog of war. Your map only fills in where you have walked. The RADIANCE skill burns the fog away.
- **Unexpected encounters:** the CODEX lists every creature as `?????` with a black silhouette until you meet it.
- **Unsolved mystery:** 8 message-bottle pages are hidden across the island and its dungeons. Read together in the JOURNAL, they reveal who you really are.
- **Hidden secrets:** red X marks hide buried treasure. You need the shovel to dig them up. Treasure chests are tucked into the island's corners.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard / Mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / Arrow keys | D-pad / Left stick |
| Sword (3-hit combo) | X / Left click (aims at mouse) | X |
| Dash (brief invulnerability) | Space / Shift / Right click | B |
| Skills: Whirlwind, Sun Bolt, Radiance | Q, E, Tab | LB, RB, Y |
| Talk / Interact | F / Enter | A |
| Lantern Wheel (pause menu) | Esc / M | Start |
| Menus: confirm / back | Enter, Space, Z / Esc, Backspace, X | A / B |
| Naming your hero | Type on the keyboard; Enter signs, Backspace erases | D-pad grid |

## Features
- A detailed 128×96 island built from seeded noise, plus hand-placed landmarks. It has autotiled coastlines, animated water, dirt paths with boardwalk bridges, a village, a lagoon, jungle, ash highlands, a cliff cave and a lighthouse.
- Two procedurally laid-out dungeons with a key branch, a locked boss door, torch-lit darkness and boss gates.
- Fast combat: combos, lunges, hit-stop, screen shake, knockback, crits, damage numbers, afterimage dashes and telegraphed enemy attacks.
- 7 shadow enemy types and 3 multi-phase bosses (The Tide King, the Grove Tyrant and The Nameless). Each boss gets enraged at 50% HP.
- **Lantern Wheel** pause menu: eight sigils orbit a burning lantern, and you turn the wheel to choose a section. Choosing one slides the wheel aside and spins up an **inner ring** of that section's own options: your items, creatures, lost pages, journals, settings, and landmarks on the chart. The chosen option points at a detail panel, Z acts on it, and X steps back out. "Leave Isle" returns you to the title screen.
- **Night on the island.** The overworld is moonlit and dark. Your lantern, torches, campfires, glowing bottles and spirit villagers are the light. Shadow creatures glow faintly so you can always read a fight.
- **Solid world.** You, enemies, wandering NPCs and animals all collide. Nobody walks through anybody. A safety net snaps you back if anything ever pushes you into water or a wall, and treasure and enemies only spawn where you can actually walk.
- **The hero** is an original muscular island warrior (generated by `tools/make_hero.py`). He has frame-by-frame idle and walk animation in four directions.
- **Good souls.** Every friendly NPC is the glowing, wandering soul of a remembered islander. They drift around their spot, stop and face you when you approach, and never walk into you.
- **Three save journals.** Inscribe into any of them from the menu, or into your current one at a campfire. From the title you can continue, open any journal, or begin a new journey in a chosen slot.
- Ink-scroll dialogue with speaker captions and inline choices, a name-signing page, and fading location captions.
- Levels and XP, MP skills with cooldowns, loot drops, healing fruit, and campfire checkpoints that heal and save.
- All music and sound effects are synthesized in code at runtime. There are no audio files.

## Run it
Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7 and press **F5**.

Developer smoke test, which loads every area, fights every boss and saves screenshots:
`godot --path . -- --autotest --shots=C:/some/folder`
(Note: this writes a test save to the game's user-data folder.)
